Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Docker and DBFilter deployment?

Docker: Each subscription runs in its own isolated container with dedicated resources. Best for customers who need custom modules, Git integration, Code Server, and full isolation.
DBFilter: Multiple subscriptions share a single Odoo instance, filtered by database. Lighter on resources, easier to manage, ideal for simpler use cases.

Will I get support after purchasing this module?

Yes! You will receive 36 hours of free support after purchase, distributed over 6 months (6 hours monthly). This includes installation, configuration, and usage assistance.

What level of experience is required?

Basic system administration knowledge is helpful but not required. The module provides an intuitive interface, and our support team will help you get started.

Which Odoo versions are supported?

The master module runs on Odoo 18, but you can manage subscriptions running on Odoo 14, 15, 16, 17, and 18+. Multi-version support is built-in.

How many servers can I manage?

Unlimited! You can manage any number of root servers and subscriptions without restrictions.

Do you support customization?

Yes! Contact us at support@sadeem.cloud to discuss your customization requirements.

Will I receive free updates?

Yes! Log in to your Odoo account and download the latest version anytime.
